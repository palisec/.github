# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **admin@palisec.tech** or send a direct message to [@palisecagents on X](https://x.com/palisecagents), as published at https://palisec.tech/.well-known/security.txt. Please do not open a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the affected repository, package version or address. Reports are acknowledged within three working days, reproduced, and fixed with a regression test. The fix and its scope are noted in the changelog (https://palisec.tech/changelog) once released.

## Scope

All repositories in the Palisec organization, the published packages (`@palisec/sdk`, `palisec`, `policy-enforced-accounts`), the API at palisec.tech and Palisec's contracts on Robinhood Chain.
